The first weeks with a kitten are a mix of chaos, 3am zoomies, and moments so sweet you forget
about the scratched furniture. A little preparation makes the transition easier on everyone —
kitten included.

## Setting the first few weeks up right

- **Start with one small room.** A whole house is overwhelming on day one. Give your kitten a
  quiet space with food, water, a litter box, and a hiding spot, then expand from there.
- **Litter box basics.** Uncovered, low-sided, and away from food and noisy appliances — kittens
  are picky about all three.
- **Let them come to you.** Especially with kids in the house, sitting quietly nearby beats
  chasing a scared kitten around the room.
- **Book the first vet visit early.** Vaccinations, deworming, and a general health check should
  happen within the first couple of weeks.

Hiding, hissing, and general suspicion of everyone are all normal at first. Give it time — most
kittens go from cautious to curious to fully in charge of the house faster than you'd expect.
