A tiny cape, a bandana, a Halloween costume that took way too long to get on — dressing up a pet
is one of those things that's either adorable or a bit much, depending on who you ask. The truth
is, it can be both fine and fun, as long as you're paying attention to the right things.

## How to tell if your pet is actually okay with it

- **Can they move normally?** No costume should restrict walking, sitting, or lying down.
- **Can they see, hear, and breathe freely?** Anything covering the eyes, ears, or muzzle for too
  long is a hard no.
- **Watch the body language.** Freezing in place, tail tucked, ears pinned back, or trying to
  chew the costume off are all signs to call it quits.
- **Keep sessions short.** A few minutes for photos is very different from wearing something all
  day.

If your pet trots around like nothing's different — or better yet, seems to be enjoying the
attention — you're in good territory. If not, no cape is worth the stress. Save it for the next
willing model.
