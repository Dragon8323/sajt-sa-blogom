Parrots aren't background pets. They're loud, curious, opinionated, and smart enough to notice
when you're ignoring them — which means bringing one home changes the rhythm of a house more than
people expect. Before getting one, it helps to know what you're actually signing up for.

## Popular species for a home

- **Budgerigar (budgie)** — small, low-maintenance, and quick to pick up a few words. A solid
  choice for first-time bird owners.
- **Cockatiel** — gentle and affectionate, easy to recognize by its crest, and happiest when it's
  part of whatever you're doing.
- **African grey** — one of the most intelligent parrots, an excellent talker, but needs serious
  daily attention and mental stimulation.
- **Macaw / cockatoo** — big, social, and deeply bonded to their people — also the loudest and the
  biggest time commitment on this list.

## Feeding

- A mix of quality seeds and pellets, not just one or the other
- Fresh fruit and vegetables — apple, carrot, spinach
- Clean water every single day
- Avoid chocolate, avocado, and alcohol — all toxic to birds

## What they need at home

- **A cage big enough to fully spread their wings** — not just big enough to stand in
- **Toys** for mental stimulation: swings, mirrors, things to chew
- **Daily time outside the cage**, supervised, when it's safe to do so
- **A draft-free spot** and a regular cleaning routine

## The upside

Parrots are genuinely rewarding — they're social, they bond hard with their owner, they can learn
tricks and vocabulary, and there's rarely a dull moment once one moves in.

## What to weigh first

- They can be loud — some species very much so
- They make a mess: feathers, scattered seed, the occasional chewed cable
- They need daily attention and don't do well left alone for long stretches
- Larger species are a real expense to keep properly

Parrots don't do well in isolation, so plan for either a pair or a lot of your own time. And think
carefully before an impulse buy — some species live 50+ years, which means a parrot bought today
could easily outlive the person who bought it.
