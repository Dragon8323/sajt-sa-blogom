Welcome to Pet Corner! This blog is about life with pets — the good, the messy, and everything
in between.

## What you'll find here

I'm planning to write two kinds of posts:

- **Personal stories** — the funny habits, the hard days, the little moments that make sharing a
  home with an animal so worth it.
- **Practical tips** — things I've learned (sometimes the hard way) about feeding, training, and
  everyday care for dogs, cats, and other pets.

Posts are tagged so you can filter by topic on the [blog page](/blog) — things like `Dogs`,
`Cats`, `Health`, or `Training` will show up as new posts get added.

That's it for the introduction. More stories and tips coming soon.
