Working from home sounded like it would mean more focus. In practice, it means a dog parked next
to the laptop, watching the cursor move like it owes them an explanation, and reminding you every
couple of hours that 2pm is, in fact, walk time.

It's not exactly efficient. Every notification gets a head tilt, every video call gets at least
one bark at the mail carrier, and somehow the keyboard is always slightly less accessible than it
should be. But there's something steadying about it too — a second heartbeat in the room, mostly
asleep, mostly content, occasionally very sure your lunch break should start now.

Turns out "productivity" and "a dog leaning against your leg all day" aren't as incompatible as
they sound.
