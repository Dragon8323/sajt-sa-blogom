Some days, the most useful thing you can do is sit down on the floor and let your dog lean into
you for a few minutes. No agenda, no training, no walk to get through — just weight against
weight, and a tail that won't stop moving.

It's easy to underestimate how much that does. Dogs don't ask how your day was or wait for you
to explain why you're tired. They just show up, tongue out, entirely unbothered by whatever's
stressing you out, and somehow that's exactly what helps.

If you've had one of those days, skip the scrolling and go find your dog instead. Five minutes on
the floor tends to work better than most things people pay for.
